How to sign a PDF with SignFlow

Open a document, add what it needs, and download a signed copy. This guide walks through the complete workflow on a phone or computer.

Open the PDF editor →

1. Open your PDF

Choose Upload PDF on desktop or Open a PDF on mobile, then select a PDF from your device. SignFlow shows a preview of the first page. Use the arrows above the document to move between pages.

Your document is processed locally in your browser. There is no account to create and no document upload to an application server. You need an internet connection to load the app's PDF libraries.

2. Create a handwritten or typed signature

Choose Sign. Select Draw to write your signature with a finger or mouse, or Type to enter your name. If you need to start over while drawing your signature, use Clear.

Choose Place signature to add it to the document. Tap the page to position it, or drag the signature into place. Use its corner handle to adjust the size.

3. Press Done to keep the position

When the signature is where you want it, press Done. The editing controls disappear, and the signature stays on that page. Tap it again if you need to adjust it.

Finish or delete an unfinished item before starting another signature, text item, date, or drawing session. If an unconfirmed signature, text item, or date is still being positioned, it follows page changes until you press Done.

4. Add text and dates

Use Text for a name, note, or other new text annotation. Enter the text, choose a size, and select Place text. Move it into position and press Done.

Use Date to choose a date, format, and size. The Today button selects today's date. Place the date and press Done just as you would for a signature. To change an existing text item or date, select it and choose Edit.

These tools add content on top of the PDF. They do not rewrite its existing text or automatically fill its form fields.

5. Draw directly on a page

Choose Draw to set the pen color, pen size, or eraser size. On mobile, tap Draw again or Close to start using those settings. The size indicator shows the area your pen or eraser will affect.

A drawing session can contain several strokes. The eraser cuts only the touched parts of strokes. Undo stroke restores a drawing or erasing action. Choose Done to keep the session, or Delete drawing to discard its changes while keeping earlier confirmed drawings.

6. Save and check your signed PDF

Choose Save on mobile or Save PDF beside the preview on desktop. Open the downloaded file to check every page before sharing it.

Only confirmed work is included. A new unfinished signature, text item, date, or drawing is left out. An existing item being edited keeps its last confirmed version in the download until you press Done again.

Documents and edits are held in memory. Download your work before closing or refreshing the page.

Working on a phone

The mobile toolbar keeps Sign, Date, Text, Draw, and Save at the bottom of the screen. Settings open in a panel that you can close to return to the document. Use two fingers to zoom the PDF, and move around the zoomed document to reach the part you need.

If something does not work

  • If PDF tools fail to load, check your internet connection and reload before opening a file.
  • If an item is missing from the download, return to the editor and press Done on that item.
  • If opening another PDF fails, the current document and edits remain available.
  • If a recipient requires a certificate-backed digital signature, use a tool that supports that requirement. SignFlow adds a visible electronic signature.

For a reproducible problem, see the contact page. Do not post confidential documents in a public issue.
