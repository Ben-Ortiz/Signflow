Questions about signing PDFs

Need a hand?
Is SignFlow free to use without signing up?

Yes. You can open, sign, annotate, and download PDFs without an account or subscription.

Can I sign a PDF on my phone?

Open SignFlow in your phone's browser and choose a PDF from your files. The mobile layout puts Sign, Date, Text, Draw, and Save along the bottom. Use two fingers to zoom the document, and press Done after placing an item.

Can I add text or a date to a PDF?

Yes. Choose Text to add a new text item, or Date to choose a date and format. Position the item and press Done before saving. These tools add annotations; they do not change the PDF's existing text.

Where do my PDF and signature go?

Your PDF and edits are processed in browser memory and are not uploaded to an application server. The app loads PDF libraries from Cloudflare's CDN, so an internet connection is needed. Save before closing or refreshing the page; unsaved work is not stored between sessions.

Is this a certificate-backed digital signature?

No. SignFlow adds a visible electronic signature to your PDF. It does not create a certificate-backed digital signature. Check the recipient's signing requirements for your document.
