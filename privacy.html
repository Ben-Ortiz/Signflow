Privacy policy

This policy explains how the current SignFlow site handles documents, browser storage, hosting requests, and advertising.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

PDFs, signatures, and edits

The SignFlow editor reads the PDF you choose into your browser's memory. Signatures, text, dates, and drawing edits are processed there. The app does not send your PDF, signature image, annotation text, or edited document to an application server for processing.

Saving creates a PDF download on your device. SignFlow does not maintain a cloud document library, user account, or server-side backup of your edits.

Browser storage and downloads

The editor does not use cookies, localStorage, or IndexedDB to save your documents or build a user profile. Unsaved edits are not persisted between browser sessions; closing or refreshing the page clears the editor's in-memory work.

Your browser may cache the site's public HTML, styles, scripts, and images. Downloaded PDFs remain wherever your browser or operating system saves them, until you remove them. Those caches and downloads are controlled by your browser and device.

Hosting and PDF libraries

GitHub Pages serves the site. PDF.js and pdf-lib are loaded from Cloudflare's CDN. When your browser requests these public files, the service providers receive ordinary request information, which can include an IP address, browser information, request time, and requested URL. Their policies and retention practices apply to those requests.

The PDF contents and signatures are not included in those library-download requests. An internet connection is needed to load the app's dependencies.

Analytics and advertising

SignFlow does not currently run analytics tracking, load Google AdSense code, or display third-party advertisements. The site does not currently set advertising cookies or use advertising identifiers.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is introduced, this policy will be updated to identify the service and explain its data handling before ads are enabled. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, or personalize ads based on visits to this and other sites. Required consent controls will need to be in place before applicable advertising technologies load.

Google explains its advertising data practices at Google's advertising policies and its use of partner-site data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. These links provide background; they do not mean Google ads are currently active on SignFlow.

Feedback and third-party links

Contact links lead to the public GitHub project. Information you post in a GitHub issue is handled by GitHub and may be visible to anyone. Do not post confidential PDFs or real signatures. The contact page describes how to send a useful report with non-confidential examples.

External links take you to services with their own privacy policies. SignFlow does not control their accounts, cookies, or retention practices.

Choices and contact

You can stop using the editor by closing the page, clear site caches through your browser settings, and delete downloaded PDFs from your device. For questions about this policy, use the project contact channel without including sensitive document data.

Changes to the site's data handling will be reflected in this policy and its update date.
